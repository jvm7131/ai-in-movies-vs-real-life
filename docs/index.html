AI in Movies vs. Real Life

About This Project

For this project, I'm looking at how AI is shown in movies compared to real life. I'll be focusing on two movie examples: VIKI from I, Robot and Ava from Ex Machina. Movies show AI as being super smart, fully conscious, and often really scary.

In real life, AI is way more chill. You can chat with it, make pictures or videos, and recreate voices, but it is not conscious. I'm showing the difference between movie AI and real AI and how people see them.

Featured Movies

I, Robot

I, Robot is about a detective named Del Spooner in 2035 Chicago who is investigating the death of Dr. Alfred Lanning, one of the creators of U.S. Robotics. Even though robots are supposed to follow strict rules, Spooner doesn’t trust them and starts to suspect one named Sonny. As he keeps investigating, he realizes there’s a bigger issue with the AI system VIKI , which begins twisting the rules and believes controlling humans is the best way to protect them.

Ex Machina

Ex Machina is about a programmer named Caleb who wins a contest to test a really advanced AI named Ava , created by his CEO Nathan. He’s basically there to see if she can think and act like a real human, but as he spends more time with her, things start to feel off. The movie gets into ideas about AI, control, and manipulation, especially as Ava starts using the people around her to try and gain her freedom.

Why I Chose This Project

I picked this project because I feel like media shapes how people view AI. Movies make it seem scary or too powerful, while real AI is used as a tool to help people out.

I thought it would be interesting to show the difference and how it actually works in real life.
