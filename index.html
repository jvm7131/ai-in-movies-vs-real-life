AI in Movies vs. Real Life

About this project

For this project, I'm loking at how AI is shown in movies compared to real life. I'll focusing on two movie examples VIKI from I, Robot & Ava from Ex Machina. Movies shows AI being super smart, fully conscious, and often really scary. In real life AI is way more chill you can chat with it, make pictures or videos, recrate vocies, but it 'is not conscious. I'm showing the difference between movie AI and real AI and how people see them.

Why I Chose This Project

I picked this project because I feel as though media shapes how people views AI. they make it seem scary or too powerful, while real AI is used a tool to help people out. I thought it would be intresting to show the differnce and how it actually works IRL.
